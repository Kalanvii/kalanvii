Maison Heritage & Philosophy

The KALANVII Legacy of Splendor

Founded in 2026, KALANVII (कलान्वी) was born out of a profound reverence for India's royal textile heritage and master artisan communities. Our name symbolizes the harmonious confluence of classical art (*Kala*) and refined wisdom (*Anand/Nidhi*).

Every potli, batua, and crescent minaudiere in our atelier is a labor of intense devotion. Passing through the skilled hands of master karigars whose families have perfected zardozi and moti-stringing across generations, each piece requires upwards of 48 hours of meticulous needlework.

The Three Pillars of KALANVII

  • ✦ Uncompromising Authenticity: Genuine Japanese micro-seed pearls and anti-tarnish electroplated brass frames.
  • ✦ Direct Atelier Pricing: Eliminating middlemen to bring heirloom luxury directly to global brides and connoisseurs.
  • ✦ Bespoke Concierge: Personalized color and bead matching for bridal lehengas and festive evening gowns.
